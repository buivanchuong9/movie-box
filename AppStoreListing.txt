DESCRIPTION
Movie Box is a private shelf for the films you care about. Add a poster from your photo library or from an image file, and it becomes a title in your library. Nothing is streamed, and Movie Box does not download a movie catalog.

Browse what you added on Home, Discover, and Search. Open a title for its poster, overview, and the lists you keep: watchlist, favorites, and watched. Search looks only through the titles on your device.

Lumen Plus unlocks the full experience after a short introduction. Choose Monthly, Annual, or Lifetime. Plus adds advanced filters, recommendations shaped by the genres you pick, and statistics for what you have watched. You can restore a purchase from Settings and manage a subscription in your Apple ID.

Your posters, lists, searches, and preferences stay on this iPhone. There is no Movie Box account. Delete the app whenever you want the library removed.

REVIEW NOTE
Movie Box does not stream or download movies. There is no account and no sign-in.

1. On first launch, swipe through the introduction. You can tap Skip, then Not now, to finish it. The posters on these screens are bundled in the app. They are not loaded from the internet.
2. The next screen is the paywall. A purchase is required before the library opens. Use a Sandbox Apple ID.
3. Products:
   - Monthly: com.lumen.discovery.plus.monthly ($3.99 / month in the US store configuration)
   - Annual: com.lumen.discovery.plus.annual ($24.99 / year)
   - Lifetime: com.lumen.discovery.plus.lifetime ($29.99, one-time, non-consumable)
4. After purchase, the library is empty until you add images. On Home, tap the plus button. On Discover, tap Import. Choose Photo Library or Files. You can select more than one image. Photos are named Movie 1, Movie 2, and so on. Files keep the file name.
5. Those titles then appear on Home, Discover, Search, and the detail screen. You can save them to a watchlist, favorites, or watched.
6. Settings includes Restore Purchases and, for an active subscription, Manage Subscription. Statistics and the advanced year, rating, language, and country filters are part of Lumen Plus.
7. In-app Lumen Plus messages are our own placements. There is no third-party advertising SDK.
8. Deleting the app removes the imported images and lists. The app does not use non-exempt encryption.

PRIVACY
Privacy Policy
Movie Box
Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what Movie Box does with information on your device. Movie Box is published for the iOS app with bundle ID com.lumen.discovery.

What the app is
Movie Box lets you import poster images and keep a personal film library. It does not stream movies, and it does not require an account.

Information stored on your device
When you use Movie Box, the following stays on your iPhone:

- Poster images you import from the photo picker or from files you choose
- Titles created from those images
- Watchlist, favorites, watched titles, and episode progress
- Recent searches and recently viewed titles
- Genre preferences chosen during introduction
- Appearance, content language, and notification preferences
- A local cache of images the app has already opened

Movie Box does not upload this library to a server we operate. We do not run a Movie Box account system, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

Photos and files
Import uses the system photo picker and the system file picker. Movie Box receives only the images you select for that import. It does not ask for ongoing access to your entire photo library. File access is limited to the files you pick.

Purchases
Lumen Plus is sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether Monthly, Annual, or Lifetime is active. Movie Box does not receive your card number. Subscription status is handled by your Apple ID. You can restore purchases in Settings.

What we do not do
- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not use a third-party advertising network or a tracking SDK.
- We do not build an advertising profile of you.
- We do not download a movie catalog from the internet in this version of the app.
- Messages about Lumen Plus inside the app are our own product messages.

Notifications
If you turn notifications on, that choice is stored on the device. Movie Box does not send a notification digest from a server.

Children
Movie Box is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

How to delete your information
Delete Movie Box from your iPhone. That removes the imported images, lists, searches, and preferences stored by the app. Purchases remain with your Apple ID and can be managed in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

SUPPORT
Movie Box Support
https://sites.google.com/view/movie-box-app/support

Movie Box helps you keep a personal film shelf on your iPhone. You import poster images. The app does not stream movies and does not use an account.

Add a title
1. Finish the introduction.
2. Purchase Lumen Plus, or restore an earlier purchase.
3. On Home, tap the plus button. On Discover, tap Import.
4. Choose Photo Library or Files.
5. Select one or more images.
Photos are saved as Movie 1, Movie 2, and so on. A file keeps its file name, without the extension.

Find a title
Home shows what you imported. Discover is the same library in a grid. Search matches the titles on this device only.

Lists
Open a title to add it to your watchlist, favorites, or watched list. Those lists stay on the device.

Lumen Plus
Monthly, Annual, and Lifetime are sold by Apple. If the app asks you to subscribe again after you already paid, open Settings in Movie Box and tap Restore Purchases. To cancel a renewing plan, open iPhone Settings, tap your Apple ID, then Subscriptions. Lifetime does not renew.

Remove your library
Delete Movie Box. Imported images, lists, and preferences stored by the app are removed with it. This does not cancel an Apple subscription.
